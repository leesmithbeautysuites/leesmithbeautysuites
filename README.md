## About LeeSmith Beauty Suites

**LeeSmith Beauty Suites** is a beauty-suite concept designed for independent professionals who want greater control over their business, workspace and client experience.

Instead of operating as a traditional salon, LeeSmith provides private professional suites where beauty and wellness entrepreneurs can operate their own independent businesses.

Our role is to provide the space, environment and infrastructure.

The professional remains in control of their:

- Brand
- Clients
- Schedule
- Services
- Pricing
- Business operations

---

## Our Business Model

LeeSmith Beauty Suites operates on a simple model:

**LeeSmith provides the suite.  
The professional builds the business.**

Independent professionals lease private suites designed for beauty and wellness services.

This creates a professional environment where entrepreneurs can work independently without the traditional commission-based salon structure.
